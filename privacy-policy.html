Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

This policy explains what information The Portfolio Briefcase collects, why, and how it's used. If anything here is unclear, reach out at contact@theportfoliobriefcase.com.

1. Information we collect

When you subscribe, we collect:

  • Your name — used to greet you in emails and on the site.
  • Your email address — used to send the newsletter, log you in, and contact you about your account.
  • Your portfolio (the tickers you choose to track) — used to personalize the content you receive.

We don't collect payment information, government ID, or anything beyond what's needed to run the newsletter.

2. How we use it

  • Sending you the daily newsletter based on your chosen tickers
  • Sending one-time login codes so you can securely access your account without a password
  • Letting you view, edit, or delete (unsubscribe) your own data
  • Responding if you contact us through the Feedback page or email

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or share it with data brokers.

3. Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to actually run the site. Each only receives the minimum data it needs to do its job:

  • Brevo — our email delivery provider. Receives your email address (and name) to send the newsletter and login codes.
  • Google Sheets — where subscriber records (name, email, portfolio, status) are stored.
  • Finnhub — our market data provider. Receives ticker symbols to look up prices and news; does not receive your name, email, or any personal information.
  • Anthropic (Claude) — generates the newsletter write-ups. Receives ticker symbols, prices, and public news headlines; does not receive your name, email, or any personal information.

These providers are only permitted to use your data to provide services to us, not for their own purposes.

4. Cookies & local storage

When you log in, we store a session token in your browser's local storage so you stay logged in between visits. This token lives only on your device and is used to identify your session when the site talks to our backend. We don't use tracking cookies or third-party analytics/advertising scripts.

5. Your choices

  • Edit your portfolio anytime from the Edit Portfolio page.
  • Unsubscribe anytime from the Unsubscribe page — this stops all future emails. Your data is retained (marked inactive) in case you resubscribe later, but you can request full deletion by emailing us.
  • Request deletion of your data entirely by contacting contact@theportfoliobriefcase.com.

6. Data security

Login is handled through one-time email codes rather than passwords, and account actions (viewing or editing your portfolio, unsubscribing) require a valid logged-in session — not just knowledge of your email address. That said, no system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security of data transmitted online.

7. Children's privacy

The Portfolio Briefcase is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll remove it.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes meaningfully, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the service after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: contact@theportfoliobriefcase.com.
